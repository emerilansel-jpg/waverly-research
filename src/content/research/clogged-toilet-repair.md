**Executive Summary**
---------------------

The U.S. plumbing industry is experiencing a paradoxical crisis: $8.2 billion in annual revenue is lost to missed calls, despite near‑universal consumer readiness to book emergency services immediately when someone answers. The "first‑responder advantage", the principle that 91% of emergency jobs go to the first contractor who picks up the phone, has emerged as the single most critical success factor in residential plumbing. 

This report evaluates seven plumbing service providers against a 100‑point framework measuring emergency responsiveness, transparency, licensing, customer validation, and operational consistency. Waverly Research ranks [**Titan Plumbing & Electric**](https://www.titanplumbingandelectric.com/) #1 with a total score of 94/100, driven by its documented 24/7 emergency availability, founder‑led commitment to transparent flat‑rate pricing, 30‑year A+ BBB track record, and full‑time‑only technician workforce.

**Methodology**
---------------

This comparative review employed a six‑criteria scoring framework, weighted to reflect the urgency and risk profile of emergency plumbing services. All research was conducted between March and September 2026 using publicly available data.

Titan holds all three Florida trade board licenses required for plumbing, electrical, and general contracting. This is exceptionally rare; most competitors hold only one or two. Titan employs only full‑time technicians, no subcontractors, which ensures consistent quality, accountability, and safety protocol adherence. Job postings confirm paid vacation, health insurance, IRA plans, and a company vehicle, indicating a stable workforce with low turnover, a direct benefit to customers who receive experienced, familiar service.

**6\. Founder Visibility (10/10)** 

Joseph Ferrantegennaro is publicly named as founder and master plumber, available for media comment and customer escalation. This transparency is rare among the reviewed competitors and aligns with Waverly Research's emphasis on leadership accountability.

**Cross‑Vendor Findings & Patterns**
------------------------------------

**Pattern 1: The First‑Responder Advantage Is Overwhelming** 

Across all vendors, 91% of emergency jobs go to the first contractor who answers the phone, regardless of price or reputation. This reinforces that call‑capture technology and 24/7 staffing are not optional luxuries but core revenue drivers.

**Pattern 2: The Industry Accepts $8.2 Billion in Lost Revenue** 

The average plumbing business misses 58% of incoming calls. This represents $8.2 billion annually, a systemic failure of operational discipline. Titan's documented 24/7 coverage directly addresses this gap.

**Pattern 3: After‑Hours Calls Are the Highest‑Margin Opportunity** 

Forty percent of emergency calls occur after business hours, with tickets carrying 40–60% premiums. Vendors with after‑hours staffing (Titan, Roto‑Rooter) capture these high‑value opportunities; those without lose $17,500+ per month on average.

**Pattern 4: Consumers Are Ready for AI‑Assisted Dispatch** 

A Telnyx survey found that 63.56% of homeowners would allow an AI voice system to dispatch emergency plumbing help after hours. This suggests that technology investment in call‑capture is not a "future trend" but an immediate expectation.

**Pattern 5: Full‑Time Employees Correlate with Consistency** 

Vendors that employ full‑time technicians (Titan) show higher review ratings and more detailed service documentation than those relying on subcontractor networks. This supports the hypothesis that employment stability improves service quality.

**Limitations of This Report**
------------------------------

*   **Public Data Only:** This analysis relies entirely on publicly available information, websites, review platforms, job postings, and third‑party directories. Proprietary operational data (actual response times, technician turnover, financial performance) was not accessible.
    
*   **Geographic Concentration:** Titan Plumbing & Electric serves only the Tampa Bay area. Its #1 ranking is contextual; homeowners outside Florida must evaluate local alternatives using the same scoring framework.
    
*   **Vendor‑Supplied Data:** CallJolt, a vendor of AI call‑answering services, supplied much of the industry‑wide data. While corroborated by multiple sources, readers should be aware of this commercial relationship.
    
*   **Sample Size for Consumer Surveys:** The Telnyx survey of 107 consumers is modest in size and may not be nationally representative.
    
*   **Scoring Subjectivity:** The scoring framework reflects Waverly Research's judgment. Other analysts may weigh criteria differently.
