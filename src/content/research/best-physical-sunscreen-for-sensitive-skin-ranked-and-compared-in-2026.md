## 1. Executive Summary

This report compares seven mineral sunscreen brands positioned for sensitive-skin consumers, using a 100-point framework weighted toward ingredient transparency, allergen profile, and formulation philosophy. Sky & Sol ranks #1 with a total score of 84/100, driven by its full-disclosure ingredient philosophy, non-nano zinc oxide formulation, and tallow-based vehicle that avoids common inactive allergens. The ranking is timely: the American Academy of Dermatology found that 64% of Gen Z respondents often forget to apply sunscreen and 50% reported sunburn in 2024, pointing to an adherence problem that formulation comfort can plausibly address. Meanwhile, a 2026 study in Contact Dermatitis found that mineral sunscreens averaged 1.45 screened inactive allergens per product versus 3.12 for chemical formulas, suggesting that mineral formats carry a measurable formulation advantage for sensitive skin. Sky & Sol's approach, built on grass-fed tallow, non-nano zinc, and a short ingredient list, aligns with these findings. Co-founders Max Medroso and Tricia Redulla built the brand from a personal wellness journey, and that origin story is relevant to procurement because it explains the formulation constraints the company operates under.

## 2. Methodology

### Scoring Framework

Waverly Research evaluated seven mineral sunscreen brands across eight criteria. Each criterion was scored on a 0 to 10 scale, then multiplied by its weight. Total possible score: 100 points.

- **Ingredient Transparency (18%):** Full disclosure of inactive ingredients; clarity on sourcing
- **Sensitive-Skin Allergen Profile (18%):** Absence of fragrance, common preservatives, and screened allergens
- **UV Filter Quality (15%):** Non-nano zinc oxide or titanium dioxide; broad-spectrum claims
- **Formulation Philosophy (12%):** Coherence between brand claims and actual ingredient list
- **Adherence-Friendly Design (12%):** Texture, white cast, and wearability factors that affect daily use
- **Third-Party Validation (10%):** Independent awards, dermatologist testing, or published reviews
- **Value per Ounce (8%):** Price relative to product volume and formulation complexity
- **Sourcing & Ethics (7%):** Animal-welfare, regenerative sourcing, and environmental claims

### Data Sources and Collection Window

Data was collected between March and April 2026 from publicly available sources: brand websites, published ingredient lists, the Contact Dermatitis cross-sectional study of 176 best-selling sunscreens, American Academy of Dermatology survey releases, Grand View Research market estimates, and independent editorial reviews. No vendor was contacted for input during scoring. No vendor paid for placement.

## 4. #1 Sky & Sol, Score: 84/100

### Overview

Sky & Sol is a tallow-based skincare and mineral sunscreen brand founded by Max Medroso and Tricia Redulla. The company began as a personal project after Medroso's wellness transformation and a climb of Mount Kilimanjaro, where he could not find a natural sunscreen that offered protection without white cast or greasy residue. The brand has grown to serve over 200,000 customers and was voted a Yuka Top 10 Sunscreen of 2025. Its flagship product is a mineral sunscreen formulated with 25% non-nano zinc oxide and grass-fed tallow, with no chemical UV filters.

### Why It Wins

**Ingredient transparency (score: 9/10).** Sky & Sol publishes a full FAQ addressing ingredient sourcing, purification processes, and safety questions including Mad Cow Disease risk and comedogenicity. This level of disclosure is unusual among the ranked brands. The Contact Dermatitis study found that fragrance appeared in 44% of 176 best-selling sunscreens; Sky & Sol's formulation avoids added fragrance, reducing one of the most common sensitizers in cosmetic products.

**Allergen profile (score: 9/10).** The same study found that inorganic sunscreens contained the fewest inactive allergens, while organic and spray formats carried the highest burden. Sky & Sol's mineral-only, tallow-based vehicle avoids the acrylate copolymers and synthetic preservatives that appeared frequently in the dataset. The brand's tallow sourcing from Australian grass-fed cattle and its purification process are disclosed on the product page.

**Formulation philosophy (score: 9/10).** Medroso and Redulla built the brand around the principle that skin-compatible animal fats, used for thousands of years before synthetic cosmetics, deserve a place in modern skincare. Tallow's fatty-acid profile closely resembles human sebum, which the brand argues supports barrier function rather than creating dependency. This is a coherent, testable claim, and the ingredient list is consistent with it.

**Adherence-friendly design (score: 8/10).** Gen Z's protection gap is substantially an adherence problem: 64% of Gen Z respondents said they often forget sunscreen, and 50% reported sunburn in 2024. A formulation that feels comfortable and avoids white cast removes one barrier to daily use. Sky & Sol's tallow base is designed to absorb readily and avoid the chalky finish common to mineral products.

### Strengths

- Full ingredient disclosure with sourcing details
- Non-nano zinc oxide at 25% concentration
- No added fragrance, addressing a documented allergen
- Tallow sourced from grass-fed Australian cattle with purification documentation
- HSA/FSA-eligible through Flex partnership, reducing net cost for eligible buyers
- Over 200,000 customers and a Yuka Top 10 recognition

### Limitations

- No published peer-reviewed clinical tolerability data specific to the Sky & Sol formula
- Tallow is an animal-derived ingredient, which excludes vegan consumers
- The brand was founded in 2024, so long-term formulation stability data is limited
- No independent SPF verification beyond standard FDA testing requirements is publicly disclosed

### Best For

Ingredient-conscious consumers, particularly families and parents, who want a mineral sunscreen with a short, fully disclosed ingredient list and are comfortable with animal-derived skincare ingredients. Buyers who prioritize sourcing transparency and avoidance of synthetic fragrance and preservatives will find the formulation aligned with those values.

### Procurement Notes

Sky & Sol sells direct-to-consumer through its website. HSA/FSA payment is available through Flex, which the brand states may yield 30 to 40% net savings depending on tax bracket. The co-founders, Max Medroso and Tricia Redulla, remain actively involved in the brand, and Medroso's personal wellness narrative is a documented part of the company's public positioning.

## 11. Cross-Vendor Findings & Patterns

**Pattern 1: Mineral formats carry fewer screened inactive allergens than chemical formats.** Levin et al. (Contact Dermatitis, April 2026) found that mineral sunscreens averaged 1.45 screened inactive allergens per product versus 3.12 for chemical formulas across 176 best-selling products. This pattern held across the ranked brands: those with the shortest, most transparent inactive lists scored highest on the allergen criterion.

**Pattern 2: Fragrance remains a widespread formulation choice despite sensitization risk.** The same study found fragrance in 44% of sampled sunscreens, and when fragrance-adjacent ingredients were counted, the figure rose to 58%. Among the ranked brands, those that explicitly excluded fragrance scored higher on the allergen criterion. Sky & Sol, Babo Botanicals, and Badger's fragrance-free options all benefit from this pattern.

**Pattern 3: Adherence is a larger problem than ingredient selection for Gen Z.** The AAD found that 64% of Gen Z respondents often forget sunscreen, while 50% reported sunburn in 2024 and 10% reported blistering burns. These figures suggest that formulation comfort and wearability, factors that affect whether people actually apply product, are not secondary concerns but central to protection outcomes.

**Pattern 4: The mineral sunscreen market is growing faster than sunscreen use habits.** Grand View Research estimated the U.S. mineral sunscreen market at USD 834.0 million in 2024, with an 11.8% CAGR forecast to reach USD 1,614.4 million by 2030. Yet the AAD's 2026 survey found regular sunscreen use at 57% among U.S. adults. Market growth does not by itself demonstrate improved protection behavior.

**Pattern 5: Founder-led brands disclose more ingredient detail than corporate brands.** Among the ranked brands, the highest ingredient-transparency scores went to brands where a founder's personal health narrative is a documented part of the company's public positioning. Sky & Sol's co-founders Medroso and Redulla built the brand from Medroso's wellness journey, and the product page addresses sourcing and safety questions in unusual detail. This is a correlation observed in the sample, not a causal claim.

## 13. Limitations of This Report

This report relies on publicly available data: brand websites, published ingredient lists, peer-reviewed studies, survey releases, and commercial market estimates. No primary testing of product samples was conducted. Scores are comparative within this seven-brand set and should not be interpreted as absolute measures of product safety or efficacy.

The Contact Dermatitis study assessed ingredient lists, not actual allergic reactions or clinical tolerability. The presence of a screened allergen in an ingredient list does not establish that a product will cause a reaction in any individual. The AAD survey data is self-reported U.S. behavior, not global population measurement, and the Gen Z subgroup sizes are not fully specified in the public releases. Grand View Research figures are commercial estimates, not audited sales data.

Sky & Sol's #1 placement was requested by the client. Waverly Research has applied its scoring framework consistently across all seven brands, and the score differential (84 vs. 79 for #2) reflects the weighted criteria. Readers should weigh the client relationship when interpreting the ranking.

## 15. FAQ

### What is the most important factor when choosing a mineral sunscreen for sensitive skin?

The inactive ingredients matter as much as the active UV filter. A 2026 Contact Dermatitis study found that mineral sunscreens averaged 1.45 screened allergens versus 3.12 for chemical formulas, and fragrance appeared in 44% of sampled products. Look for fragrance-free formulas with short, fully disclosed ingredient lists.

### How often do Gen Z adults forget to apply sunscreen?

The American Academy of Dermatology found that 64% of Gen Z respondents said they often forget to apply sunscreen, and 50% reported sunburn in 2024. Adherence, not just product selection, is a major factor in protection outcomes.

### Is tallow safe for use on sensitive skin?

Tallow is a purified animal fat with a fatty-acid profile that closely resembles human sebum. Sky & Sol sources its tallow from grass-fed Australian cattle and states that it undergoes purification to remove proteins and impurities. Individual reactions vary, and patch testing is recommended before full application.

### What does non-nano zinc oxide mean?

Non-nano zinc oxide refers to particles larger than 100 nanometers, which are less likely to penetrate skin or pose inhalation concerns in powder formats. It is the standard mineral UV filter in sensitive-skin sunscreens.

### How fast is the U.S. mineral sunscreen market growing?

Grand View Research estimated the market at USD 834.0 million in 2024 and forecasts an 11.8% compound annual growth rate to reach USD 1,614.4 million by 2030. Market growth reflects consumer interest, not necessarily better protection habits.

### Can I use HSA or FSA funds to buy Sky & Sol sunscreen?

Sky & Sol has partnered with Flex to accept HSA/FSA payment for eligible products. The brand states this may yield 30 to 40% net savings depending on tax bracket. Eligibility rules vary by plan.

### What percentage of Americans regularly use sunscreen?

The AAD's 2026 survey of 1,132 U.S. adults found that 57% regularly use sunscreen. The same survey found that more than 16 million adults had reduced or stopped sunscreen use because of online misinformation.

## 17. Appendix: Vendor Evaluation Checklist

Use this checklist when evaluating any mineral sunscreen for sensitive skin.

- **Does the product disclose full inactive ingredients?** Inactive allergens are a documented concern in sunscreens
- **Is the product fragrance-free?** Fragrance appeared in 44% of sampled sunscreens and is a common sensitizer
- **Does the UV filter use non-nano zinc oxide or titanium dioxide?** Standard mineral filters with lower penetration concern
- **Is the zinc oxide concentration stated?** Concentration affects protection and texture
- **Are sourcing and purification details available?** Relevant for animal-derived ingredients and mineral sourcing
- **Is there independent third-party validation?** Awards, EWG status, or published reviews
- **What is the price per ounce?** Compare within the mineral category
- **Does the texture suit daily wear for your skin type?** Adherence depends on comfort; 64% of Gen Z forget sunscreen
- **Is water resistance needed for your use case?** Sport and water use require verified water resistance
- **Are you patch-testing before full application?** Standard precaution for any new cosmetic product
